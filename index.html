Welcome to my world!

Hey, I’m Jonathan — also known as RealAutistic06. I’m an autistic author and future web developer from Scotland who’s passionate about creativity, technology, and storytelling. This website is my little corner of the internet where I share my work, ideas, and journey toward becoming a developer.

What You’ll Find Here:

On this website, you’ll find a mix of everything that makes up who I am and what I love creating. You’ll be able to explore my books — each written from the heart and inspired by real emotion and experience — as well as my current projects in web design and development. I share insights, updates, and thoughts about my creative process, alongside behind-the-scenes looks at how my ideas come to life. You’ll also find personal reflections, creative experiments, and the steps I’m taking as I grow from an aspiring web developer into a professional one. My goal is to build not just a portfolio, but a space that feels human — honest, relatable, and full of creativity. Whether you’re here to read my stories, get inspired, or watch my journey unfold, I hope this site feels like a calm, welcoming corner of the internet.

My Mission:

To inspire, create, and make a difference — one line of code, one page, or one idea at a time. Whether you’re here to read, learn, or just explore, I hope my world gives you something to feel connected to.
