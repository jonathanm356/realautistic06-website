HOME PAGE

Welcome to my World!

Welcome. I’m Rowan, an author and creative based in Scotland. I write honest, reflective work shaped by lived experience, focusing on autism, anxiety, identity, and what it means to exist in a world that often feels overwhelming or out of step. This website is a space for my writing and creative projects, built with care and intention. It’s a quiet place to explore stories that are personal, thoughtful, and rooted in real experience.

My Work

My writing explores mental health, neurodivergence, and emotional reality without softening or exaggeration. I’m drawn to stories that sit with discomfort, ask questions, and reflect the complexity of being human. Alongside non-fiction and personal writing, I also work with fiction and psychological themes, often blending emotion and atmosphere to create pieces that feel immersive and reflective rather than purely plot-driven.

Books & Projects

I am the author of several independently published books that explore autism, anxiety, identity, and lived experience, as well as fictional work with psychological depth. Each project I release is created carefully and intentionally, with the aim of offering understanding, comfort, or connection to readers who may recognise parts of themselves in the work.

Why I Write

I write because words have always been a way to process, understand, and make sense of the world. Writing allows me to slow things down, sit with difficult experiences, and turn them into something meaningful. For me, writing isn’t about perfection or performance. It’s about honesty. If my work helps someone feel less alone, more understood, or simply seen, then it’s done what it was meant to do.

Explore
