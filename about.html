About Me

The Story Behind the Screen

Hey, I’m Jonathan — an autistic author, creative thinker, and aspiring web developer from Scotland. I’ve always been passionate about finding new ways to express myself, whether that’s through writing, design, or technology. Creativity is how I make sense of the world — it’s where I find peace, focus, and purpose. I started my journey as an author, turning personal experiences into meaningful stories that connect with others. My books explore topics like autism, anxiety, and identity — not from a place of struggle, but from a place of honesty and strength. Each one has helped me grow, and it’s my hope that they help others feel understood too. Alongside writing, I’m learning the world of coding and web development. I love the idea of combining creativity and technology to build things that can inspire or comfort people — whether that’s through visuals, words, or simple user experiences. Outside of work and projects, I’m someone who enjoys quiet spaces, good coffee, music that tells a story, and spending time with my black cat. Every day, I try to make small progress toward something meaningful — even if it’s just one more line of code, one more paragraph, or one more idea. This website is a reflection of that journey — a place to create freely, share openly, and grow at my own pace.
