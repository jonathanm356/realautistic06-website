"The Boy Who Felt Too Much"

A deeply emotional journey through the eyes of a young man who feels everything — from the chaos of sound to the quiet ache of chronic illness. Through reflections on autism, anxiety, and rare medical conditions, this book explores what it truly means to live in a world that often feels too loud, too bright, and too heavy — and how sensitivity can become a quiet kind of strength.

"Living with the What-Ifs: Life with Generalised Anxiety Disorder"

An open and relatable look into the constant tug-of-war between fear and logic. This book dives into the exhausting world of “what if” thinking — the endless scenarios, the heart-racing worries, and the quiet victories that come from learning to live alongside anxiety instead of fighting it. Through raw honesty and reflection, it shows that even in uncertainty, peace can be found.
