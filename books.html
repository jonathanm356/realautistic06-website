1. "The Boy Who Felt Too Much"

A deeply emotional journey through the eyes of a young man who feels everything — from the chaos of sound to the quiet ache of chronic illness. Through reflections on autism, anxiety, and rare medical conditions, this book explores what it truly means to live in a world that often feels too loud, too bright, and too heavy — and how sensitivity can become a quiet kind of strength.
